S-band advantage: Doppler rate of change at 2.5GHz is ~50 Hz/s at el=10° vs ~400 Hz/s at Ka-band. CW moves only ~5Hz during a 100ms integration window at S-band — well within the 10Hz FFT bin. Combined with SCS=15kHz CP of 4.8µs (vs 2.4µs at Ka), S-band CW-aided DRX sleep cycles can reach 30–120s — approaching terrestrial eDRX performance without any additional waveform overhead. Patent Claim Sets B and C apply directly and are stronger at S-band than Ka-band.
